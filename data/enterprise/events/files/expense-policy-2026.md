# Acme Corp — Expense Policy (2026 edition)

Effective 1 November 2026. This edition replaces the 2023 Expense Policy and the 2024 Expense Policy Update. Where they disagree with this document, this document wins.

## General Rules

- Submit business expenses within 45 days of the purchase date.
- Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
- Manager approval is required for all expenses over $150.

## Travel

- Economy class for domestic flights. Business class for international flights over 6 hours.
- Hotels: up to $230 per night for domestic travel and $330 for international travel.
- Meal per diem during travel: $85 per day domestic, $110 per day international.
- Book flights and hotels through the company-approved booking tool.

## Software and AI Tools

- Software subscriptions under $100/month can be expensed directly.
- AI tool subscriptions (for example GitHub Copilot or ChatGPT Plus) are pre-approved up to $40/month.
- Subscriptions over $250/month need VP approval and must go through procurement.
- All software must pass IT Security review before installation on company devices.

## Equipment and Home Office

- Additional equipment (monitors, keyboards, ergonomic accessories) up to $500 can be expensed with manager approval.
- One-time home office setup allowance: $1,500 for remote employees.
- Annual home office maintenance budget: $400 per year.

## Team Events

- Team meals and events: up to $90 per person.
- Offsites and team-building activities must be pre-approved by the department VP.
- Client entertainment: up to $150 per person with prior manager approval.

## Reimbursement

- Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.
- Questions about expense status go to finance@acmecorp.com.
