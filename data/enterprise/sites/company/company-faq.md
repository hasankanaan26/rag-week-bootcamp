# Acme Corp — Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I work from another country?

Remote work is allowed within the employee's country of residence. International remote work requires VP approval, a tax review by Finance, and may be limited to 30 days per year. Contact hr@acmecorp.com for details.

## What happens if I don't use all my vacation days?

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year. Any remaining days expire on December 31. Sick leave does not carry over. See leave-policy.md for the full policy.

## Who do I contact about IT issues?

Email it-support@acmecorp.com or use the #it-help Slack channel. For urgent issues (e.g., account lockout, security incidents), call the IT hotline at ext. 4357. Response time is typically within 2 hours during business hours.

## How do I get access to a new tool or software?

Software under $75/month can be expensed directly. For anything more expensive, submit a request through the IT self-service portal. All software must pass IT Security review before installation on company devices. See expense-policy.md for the full approval process.

## Can I expense my home internet bill?

Acme Corp does not reimburse home internet costs directly. However, remote employees receive a one-time $1,500 home office setup allowance that can be used for internet equipment (router, mesh system, etc.). See expense-policy-2024-update.md for current amounts.

## What is the dress code?

Acme Corp has a casual dress code for regular work days. Business casual is expected for client meetings and company events. There is no formal written policy — use good judgment.

## How do I report a security concern?

Email security@acmecorp.com immediately. For urgent issues, call the IT hotline at ext. 4357 and select option 2. All security reports are treated confidentially. Do not discuss potential security incidents in public Slack channels.

## Where can I find the engineering wiki?

The engineering wiki is hosted on Confluence at wiki.internal.acmecorp.com. New employees get access during Week 2 of onboarding. If you can't access it, contact your team lead or file a ticket in #it-help.
