# Acme Corp — Engineering Salary Bands (2026)

Confidential. Owner: People Operations. Do not forward outside HR and people managers.

## Individual Contributors

- Junior Developer (L1): $78,000 to $95,000 per year.
- Developer (L2): $95,000 to $120,000 per year.
- Senior Developer (L3): $120,000 to $155,000 per year.
- Staff Engineer (L4): $155,000 to $190,000 per year.

## Engineering Managers

- Engineering Manager (M1): $150,000 to $185,000 per year.
- Senior Engineering Manager (M2): $180,000 to $220,000 per year.

## How Bands Are Used

- Offers start at the band midpoint unless the hiring manager's VP approves an exception.
- Bands are reviewed every January against market data.
- Promotions move an employee to at least the minimum of the new band.
