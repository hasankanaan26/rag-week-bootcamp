# Acme Corp — Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility

- All engineering roles are eligible for fully remote or hybrid work.
- New employees must work on-site for their first 2 weeks for onboarding.
- Remote work arrangements must be documented and approved by your manager.

## Core Hours

- All employees must be available during core hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local timezone.
- Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule.
- Team meetings should be scheduled during core hours whenever possible.

## Communication Expectations

- Respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during core hours.
- Email response time: within 1 business day.
- Keep your Slack status updated to reflect your availability.
- Use video for all scheduled meetings. Camera-on is encouraged but not required.

## Home Office Requirements

- Employees must have a stable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download).
- A quiet, dedicated workspace is required for meetings.
- Company equipment must be used for all work-related tasks.
- Home office must comply with basic ergonomic guidelines (see HR portal for details).

## Security

- Always use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Lock your screen when stepping away from your workstation.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for work without the VPN active.
- Report any security incidents to security@acmecorp.com immediately.

## In-Office Days

- Hybrid employees are expected in the office a minimum of 2 days per week.
- Teams may designate specific "anchor days" when all members are in the office.
- Hot desking is available — reserve your desk through the office booking system.

## Time Zones

- Acme Corp operates across multiple time zones.
- When scheduling cross-timezone meetings, rotate meeting times to share the burden.
- Document decisions in writing so teammates in other time zones can stay informed.
