# Acme Corp — New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Week 1: Getting Started

- Collect your laptop and access badge from the IT desk on Floor 2.
- Set up your company email and Slack account using the credentials in your welcome packet.
- Complete the mandatory security awareness training on the learning portal.
- Schedule a 1-on-1 with your direct manager to discuss your first 30-day goals.
- Join the #general, #engineering, and #your-team Slack channels.

## Week 2: Systems and Tools

- Get access to GitHub by requesting it through the IT self-service portal.
- Set up your local development environment following the Engineering Wiki.
- Complete the "Intro to Our Codebase" self-paced tutorial (approximately 3 hours).
- Request access to staging and development cloud environments from your team lead.
- Set up your VPN connection for remote work using the guide in the IT knowledge base.

## Week 3: First Contributions

- Attend the weekly engineering standup (Tuesdays at 10:00 AM).
- Pick up a "good first issue" from the team backlog.
- Submit your first pull request and get it reviewed.
- Attend the monthly engineering all-hands meeting.

## Week 4: Settling In

- Complete your 30-day check-in with your manager.
- Fill out the onboarding feedback survey sent by HR.
- Set up your recurring 1-on-1 meetings with your manager (bi-weekly recommended).
- Update your profile on the company directory with your photo and role description.

## Key Contacts

- IT Support: it-support@acmecorp.com or Slack #it-help
- HR Questions: hr@acmecorp.com or Slack #hr-questions
- Engineering Onboarding Buddy Program: Ask your manager to assign you a buddy.
