# Acme Corp — Expense Policy

## General Rules

- All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.
- Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
- Expenses must be submitted through the Finance portal with the appropriate category.
- Manager approval is required for all expenses over $100.

## Travel

- Economy class flights for domestic travel. Business class for international flights over 6 hours.
- Hotel bookings should not exceed $200 per night for domestic travel or $300 for international.
- Per diem for meals during travel: $60 per day domestic, $80 per day international.
- Use the company-approved booking tool for all flight and hotel reservations.
- Rental cars require pre-approval from your manager.

## Software and Tools

- Software subscriptions under $50/month can be expensed directly.
- Subscriptions between $50 and $200/month need manager approval.
- Subscriptions over $200/month need VP approval and must go through procurement.
- All software must be reviewed by IT Security before installation on company devices.

## Equipment

- Employees receive a standard laptop and peripherals on their start date.
- Additional equipment (monitors, keyboards, ergonomic accessories) up to $500 can be expensed with manager approval.
- Home office setup allowance: one-time $1,000 budget for remote employees.

## Team Events

- Team meals and events: up to $50 per person.
- Offsites and team-building activities: must be pre-approved by the department VP.
- Client entertainment: up to $100 per person with prior manager approval.

## Reimbursement

- Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.
- Rejected expenses will include a reason and can be resubmitted with corrections.
- Questions about expense status can be directed to finance@acmecorp.com.
