# Acme Corp — Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.
Unused leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry over between years.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Public Holidays

Acme Corp observes 10 public holidays per year, aligned with the local jurisdiction.
If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed as the holiday.

## Requesting Leave

All leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.
Managers must approve or deny leave requests within 2 business days.
Emergency leave can be requested by contacting your direct manager and HR.

## Unpaid Leave

Employees may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons.
Unpaid leave must be approved by both the direct manager and the HR department.
