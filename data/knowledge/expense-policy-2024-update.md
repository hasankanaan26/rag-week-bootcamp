# Acme Corp — Expense Policy Update (2024)

## Effective Date

This update takes effect on January 1, 2024 and supersedes the corresponding sections in the main Expense Policy document.

## Updated Meal Reimbursement

- Per diem for meals during travel: $75 per day domestic (previously $60), $100 per day international (previously $80).
- Team meals and events: up to $75 per person (previously $50).
- Client entertainment: up to $125 per person with prior manager approval (previously $100).

## Updated Home Office Allowance

- Home office setup allowance increased to $1,500 for remote employees (previously $1,000).
- Annual home office maintenance budget: $300 per year for replacement peripherals and supplies (new).

## Updated Software Policy

- Software subscriptions under $75/month can be expensed directly (previously $50/month).
- AI tool subscriptions (e.g., GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT Plus) are pre-approved up to $30/month — no manager approval needed.

## Unchanged Policies

All other expense policies remain unchanged. Refer to the main Expense Policy document for complete guidelines on travel, equipment, and reimbursement procedures.
